Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Information processed

When you register, REHZO 99 processes your email, display name and country. If you publish a listing, it processes property details, contact phone/email, photos and any GPS coordinates you submit. Support tickets contain the subject and message you provide.

2. Purpose

Information is used to provide accounts, display public accommodation listings, process booking requests, provide support and protect the service.

3. Public and private information

Property name, city/area, rent, room type, amenities and listing photos may be public. Owner contact details and exact room coordinates are stored separately and are intended to be visible only to the owner and a seeker with a final-confirmed booking. Approximate coordinates may be used for nearby search.

4. Location permissions

Your browser location is requested only when you tap Find Room or Capture Room GPS. You may deny permission; manual search remains available. If you submit a room GPS location, it is stored to provide directions after booking confirmation.

5. Storage and security

Account and listing data are stored in the REHZO 99 Supabase project. Access is restricted through authentication and database policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; keep your password private and use a unique password.

6. Retention and choices

You may contact support to request help with your account or data. Booking record retention and deletion controls are still being finalised before broad public launch.

7. Payments and third parties

REHZO 99 does not currently process payments. The site uses hosting, database and authentication providers to operate.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, submit a signed-in support ticket at Support. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment PINs.
