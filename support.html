Support

REHZO 99 users can create and track support tickets after signing in to the website.

How to create a ticket

Open the website, log in, open My account and choose Create support ticket. You can view ticket status in your account dashboard.

Report a safety concern

Include the listing name or 12-digit TRL number, a short description and relevant details. Do not include passwords, OTPs, payment PINs or sensitive identity documents.

Response times

Support tickets are stored in the service. A response-time commitment and direct WhatsApp support contact have not yet been configured.
