Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. About REHZO 99

REHZO 99 helps people discover rooms, PG accommodation, hostels and flats and allows property owners to publish accommodation listings.

2. Accounts

Users must register with a valid email address and protect their password. Email confirmation may be required. You are responsible for activity performed through your account.

3. Listings and owners

Owners must publish truthful details, lawful accommodation, accurate rent, availability, photos and location. Only WebP room photos are accepted, up to three images per listing. REHZO 99 does not guarantee the accuracy of owner-provided content.

4. Booking flow

A booking request is not a confirmed booking. The owner may accept or reject it. After acceptance, the seeker must make a final confirmation. The first final confirmation is accepted by the system; a 12-digit TRL is then generated. Do not pay an owner solely because a request was accepted.

5. Privacy and location

Owner phone/email and exact room directions are intended to be disclosed only after final booking confirmation. Nearby search uses approximate listing coordinates. Browser location is requested only after you choose a location feature.

6. Payments

REHZO 99 does not currently collect rent, deposits or platform payments. Do not submit payment credentials to this website.

7. Support and enforcement

Users may submit a support ticket from their signed-in account. Listings or accounts may be restricted for abuse, fraud or unlawful content. See Support and Privacy Policy.
